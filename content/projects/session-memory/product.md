## The problem

A coding agent starts every session knowing nothing about the last one. I was re-explaining the same decisions, and good reasoning from last week was buried in transcripts nobody would read again.

## Who it is for

One developer working with agents across several projects. That is me.

## How I use it

I do nothing. Sessions are filed as they happen. When I come back to a project, the briefing is already in the session, and when I need something specific I search for it.

## In scope

Capturing, filing, tagging, summarizing and finding past sessions.

## Left out on purpose

Sharing between people, and any attempt to replace the project's own documentation.

## How success is judged

How often I have to explain something twice, and whether I can find a past decision in under a minute.

## Main risk

A memory full of noise. Filing by project and summarizing at compaction keep the useful part small.
